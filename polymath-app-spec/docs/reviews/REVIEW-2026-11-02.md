# Polymath repo review, 2 November 2026

Scope: all of `polymath-app-spec/` and `polymath-app/`, reviewed through five lenses, plus online research on learning evidence, the tech stack and compliance. No project file was changed.

**Result:** 187 findings, 52 of them high. One skeptic re-checked all 52 against the cited lines and URLs: 34 confirmed, 18 partly confirmed (usually a lower severity or a corrected detail), none refuted, and a few are duplicates. Medium and low findings were not re-checked. The full list is in the appendix.

## The verdict

The specification is unusually rigorous on paper, and its validators pass. The problem is that **they pass because they check shape, not substance**. `validate_lean_manifest` says OK, the fuzz says 200 samples passed, and the review column is stamped, yet the lean content can't do what the plan needs it to do. The other big problem is **sequencing**: 40 weeks of building before first daily use, with nothing to carry the study done in those 40 weeks into the app.

## Fix these first

### 1. Phase -1 starts this week and its retention test is invalid
- The protocol tests four generators, but two of them (`complex-division`, `derivative-product-sine`) are for skills outside the eight in scope. The week-1 skills are algebra, and none of the four generators tests them. So R1 and R2, which decide Go or Adjust, measure skills you haven't studied or never will under the protocol (`00d:80-122`).
- `ohms-law` also has a hard prerequisite, `elec.dc.charge-current-voltage`, that isn't in the eight.
- The outcome table has gaps: no row for A1 pass with A2 fail, R1 at 0.50-0.60, or A4 = 3.

**Do:** use the now-existing `tpl.math.order-*`, `solve-linear-*`, `units-*`, `rearrange-*` and `tpl.elec.ohms-law-*` templates after hand-solving them, re-test each skill 14 days after its own first study, add the missing skill, and rewrite the outcome table as first-match rules with a default.

### 2. "Verified" means nothing yet for the 30 lean templates
This is the most serious finding, because SM-06 and contract 7.8 rest on it.
- `stamp_g3_review.py` wrote the review block on all 30 templates under the name `founder`, and recorded a fuzz pass it never ran. `REVIEW-LOG.md` shows all 30 hand-solves as `pending`. The runbook says "no batch auto-approve" on line 10 and lists the stamp script as a step on line 41.
- Under `07:477`, a matching hash plus a fuzz pass makes a template `verified`.
- 22 of 30 templates have a placeholder key (`ans: 1`) and the real answer is typed option text no verifier reads. Swapping right and wrong options still passed on 22 of 22.
- Generated numeric templates verify the generator with its own expression (`r * i` against `r * i`). `independence` is false by doc 07's own definition.
- 24 of 30 produce one fixed question, so 13 of 19 skills have no fresh variants. That is the exact claim the go/no-go is meant to test, and it collides with the no-repeat rule (`06:173`).
- All 30 are `task_kind: problem`: no worked example, faded or retrieval template, and doc 07 never says how those stages are authored. `problem` is also excluded on phone, so a micro session as specified would be empty.
- 23 of 30 are multiple choice, 22 with two options. 14 of 19 skills have no constructed-response item, so mastery of nodal analysis can come from a recall card (about 1 in 8 by pure guessing).
- The 22 keys that were read are correct, so this is a process failure and not a live wrong answer.

**Do:** treat the pool as a stub. Remove all 30 stamps. Replace the script with a per-template CLI that requires your hand-solved answers. Parameterise computable items, add a `concept_static` class with mandatory human sign-off, and extend the validators to check loop stages, distinct stems and constructed-response coverage. Re-size BL-0065 and BL-0066, which are budgeted at 24 h for what is now clearly more work.

### 3. One safety error on the bench
- **L2 fault drill (fix before anyone builds it):** `project-ladder.yaml:1439` offers "fitting the flyback diode backwards". With the MOSFET on, that puts USB 5 V across a forward diode, close to a short through a breadboard. Step s11 has no safety gate. Replace it with "diode omitted", or add a current limit and a named supply, and gate the step.
- **L0 s07 teaches wrong physics:** the check says the resistor voltage drops to a tenth when R is multiplied by 10. It stays near 3.3 V minus Vf, so every correct reading is flagged. The fix is one line.

### 4. The first scheduler build will be wrong in ways the docs tell agents to preserve
- **`ts-fsrs` 6.0.0 defaults to FSRS-7.** The binding decision G11, `00e` and doc 06 say the opposite, and the config key in doc 06 is wrong. An adapter could run FSRS-7 while recording `fsrs-6`. BL-0041 wouldn't catch it, since its contract file is frozen from the adapter. Amend G11, pass `version: 'FSRS-6'`, and add an outside oracle such as py-fsrs.
- **The reference oracle has bugs,** and CLAUDE.md forbids editing vectors to fit:
  - `pack_session` interleaves untagged skills.
  - Continuation ignores `blocked_by` and decayed prerequisites.
  - Placed skills end in limbo.
  - `symbolic_equiv.py` grades a timeout or parse error as wrong, so a correct but slow answer becomes an FSRS lapse.
- **Reviews have no safe idempotency.** Reconcile would re-apply attempts every 60 s, because some attempts legitimately have no `review_log` row (`03:533` vs `06:82-84`). Add `attempts.state_applied_at`.

### 5. Security gaps to close while they are cheap
- **`schema.sql:1441` leaves `authenticated` out of the function revoke.** On Supabase, a learner may be able to call `enqueue_job`, `claim_jobs` and similar through `/rest/v1/rpc`. This wasn't run on a live project. A one-word fix.
- **FR-060 has no database gate on the serve path.** The service-role builder bypasses RLS and `session_task_pool_guard` checks only the pool. Extend the trigger.
- **The correct multiple-choice option has the id `correct`** in `rendered.options`, so all 23 multiple-choice templates expose the key to the client.
- **Spec-level conflicts:** repo evidence is fetched by the worker in doc 10, and forbidden in doc 12 and CLAUDE.md. Sandbox auth requires HMAC in doc 12 and no secret in doc 03.

### 6. Things that break on contact with today's vendors
- **Claude:** Sonnet 5.5 returns 400 on `temperature`, so doc 08 fails as written. Thinking tokens count toward `max_tokens`, so the 450-token cap can truncate replies, and the cost model omits them.
- **iOS:** magic-link-only sign-in opens Safari, not the installed PWA (third-party sources, not device-tested). Make a six-digit code the main path and test on a real iPhone.
- **Supabase:** legacy anon and service-role keys are being retired by the end of 2026, new tables need explicit grants, and the docs predate both.
- **Next.js:** the scaffold is on 15, whose security support ends 31 Jan 2027. Next 16 builds with Turbopack, which affects the Workbox plan (Serwist fits), and `middleware` is renamed `proxy`.
- **Fly:** auto-stop doesn't work over `.internal` and needs Flycast. nsjail on Fly is unproven, so run a half-day spike before D3.

## Decisions only you can make

1. **Move first use earlier? (Recommended: yes.)** Phase -1 first is right. But the riskiest hypotheses go untested for nine months, because M0 puts auth, RLS, worker, HMAC, Fly and staging ahead of a pure scheduler that needs none of them. Add a local-first Phase 0: scheduler, grading-core and verified content in a single-user PWA, and run the go/no-go on that. This is a scope change and needs a CCR. It costs a later data migration from IndexedDB. The saving was the finder's estimate and was not independently confirmed.
2. **What do you study in weeks 5-39?** No document says. The 19-skill slice runs out after roughly 7-10 weeks, and nothing imports Anki state into the app. So at week 40 the app re-teaches known material, which confounds G4 and G10. Either hold out a set of skills for the go/no-go or take decision 1, which mostly removes the problem.
3. **Is buying better than building for the math part?** The docs contain no competitor analysis. A 4-week Phase -1 arm on Math Academy for the math slice would tell you. Hardware is where nothing comparable was found, which is the real differentiator.
4. **Does the 2.5 h/week study plan define "plan" in G1?** Set it explicitly: 150 minutes in lean, 90 at 5 h/week. Passing at 2.5 h doesn't validate the 6 h steady state, so add a four-week gate at that dose before any deferred group.
5. **Add a thesis-level kill criterion?** Every rubric row in doc 11 only adjusts the design. `00d` has stop rules, but no pre-specified test against textbook plus Anki on the same delayed items.

## Measurement validity
- **EXP-001 can't say "no harm".** A 5-item pair difference has noise of about 0.25-0.29, not the assumed 0.175. Non-inferiority power at 12 pairs is then about 0.29-0.37. The Bastani -17% is relative, about 0.12 absolute, so doc 11 is wrong to call it detectable. Report intervals and forbid "no harm" wording.
- **The OFF arm is defined two ways,** and under one of them it can never reach mastery. Define it before the freeze.
- **EXP-002 can't reach its target:** it needs 8 sets, but under its own same-module rule only one pair of sets exists, of size 2.
- **Delayed tests are missing from doc 06** and would take about 25-35% of usable time at steady state.
- **The go/no-go is thin:** G4, G6 and G7 aren't decidable on four weeks of one person's data, G5 and G8 change no outcome, and there's no prescribed response to an unverified serve.
- **New evidence to add:** arXiv 2604.04721 on persistence after brief AI help (mixed across its experiments), and work that contests the attempt-first gate. Add a persistence outcome. Per-skill FSRS also has no evidence row; add Lindsey, DAS3H and Pan and Rickard.

### [high, error] Assumed pair-difference SD of 0.175 is below the measurement noise of a 5-item score

- **Verdict:** confirmed. Formula checked: sqrt(2p(1-p)/5) = 0.29 at p .7. Within-skill item correlation adds between-skill variance, so this is a floor. - **Detail:** The table cannot hold unless items are strongly informative or scores sit near the ceiling.
