# 16 — Capability outcomes (after the full spine)

Status: v1.0, 1 Nov 2026. Owner: product. **Planning document, not a contract change.** Describes credible **demonstrable** outcomes when the founder completes **T0 through T6** in `15-cyber-physical-capability-spine.md` on top of existing platform-architect skills—not Polymath v1 alone.

Evidence tags as in the contract. Role claims below are **[P]** career inference, not guarantees.

---

## 1. Headline outcome

You become someone who can take a **cyber-physical product** from idea → schematic/CAD → firmware → secure cloud/AI backend → **test evidence** → **export-aware documentation**, and own the architecture story end to end.

That is a **solo founder / principal consultant / technical lead** profile—not a line EE on a factory floor, a cleared weapons designer, or a licensed signatory for certified aviation/medical/grid safety.

---

## 2. What you could demonstrate (artifacts)

| Capability | Evidence of “done” |
|---|---|
| Build real hardware | Breadboard → PCB → assembled unit; I–V/timing/logs; debug failures |
| Write real firmware | C on MCUs; drivers; ISR/RTOS basics; sensors; motor control literacy |
| Mechanical ownership | CAD parts/assemblies; printed or outsourced parts; fit on frame/vehicle |
| Integrated system | Edge node + secure uplink + lakehouse pipeline + dashboard/alerts |
| Security architecture | Threat model; segmentation; keys; SBOM/supply chain for device + cloud |
| Production AI | RAG/agent with evals, guardrails, cost/latency SLOs—not a demo notebook |
| Dual-use literacy | Product decomposition; which EU/US categories **might** apply; classification **workflow** with counsel |
| Defense-adjacent delivery | Hardened OT-style design literacy; audit-friendly docs for integrators |

Portfolio beats certificates: repos, boards, test reports, architecture packs, eval dashboards.

---

## 3. Professional engagements (extends current consulting)

**Consulting**

- Edge-to-cloud architect for industrial, mobility, or defense **subcontractors**
- Technical lead on dual-use **components** (sensors, comms, edge AI boxes): V&V planning; interface with export/compliance teams
- OT + cloud security literacy: zones/conduits and secure remote access
- Applied AI on operational data with eval discipline and privacy/export awareness

**Building**

- UAV-class systems at **integration** level (commercial FC, custom frame/power, companion computer, mission logging)
- Field instruments or logging platforms (hardware + firmware + operated backend)
- Narrow defense-tech **supplier** posture for subsystems/software—subject to licensing and customer rules

## 4. By the five founder goals

| Goal | After full spine | Still not automatic |
|---|---|---|
| Dual-use technology | Design/build civil products with Annex I–aware specs; audit-ready technical files; review design changes with counsel when a feature may trigger control, never structure to sidestep one | Export license authority; skipping national procedures |
| Defense tech | Integrator/architect: secure comms, embedded hardening, test evidence for primes/NATO-facing supply chains | Classified design teams without clearance |
| AI projects | Own production LLM/ML features; MLOps; edge inference at the device boundary | Research novelty claims |
| Cybersecurity | Contribute to IT/OT/embedded threat modelling and remediation | Unauthorized testing |
| Real world | Personal bench debug, CAD revision, firmware flash, field test campaigns | Sign-off engineer for regulated domains |

---

## 5. Floor, middle, ceiling

| Stage | Outcome |
|---|---|
| **Floor** (Polymath v1 first walk only) | Strong embedded literacy + one proven gadget; not drone/defense/CAD-credible alone |
| **Middle** (v1 + partial spine: physical + OT cyber + cloud stack) | Credible **cyber-physical principal** for industrial and dual-use **components** |
| **Ceiling** (full spine + years of projects + counsel relationships) | **Integrated prototype one-person army** and architecture on regulated programs—still bounded by law, clearance, and unspecialized domains (EW, aerostructures certification, etc.) |

---

## 6. Market positioning (one sentence)

From “I design enterprise data platforms” to “I design **data and AI platforms connected to physical systems I’ve built and tested**, with security and export-aware engineering.”
