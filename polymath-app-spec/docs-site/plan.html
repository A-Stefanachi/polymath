Phase −1 (pre-build)

Phase −1 measures whether the founder can sustain approximately 2.5 hours per week of structured practice across four weeks, with delayed retrieval of week-one material in weeks three and four. The same activity supports learning during subsequent implementation.

  1. Select approximately eight skills from the lean curriculum (algebra and DC electronics fundamentals).
  2. Run FSRS scheduling in Anki; use generated problem sheets from reference/verifiers.
  3. Record adherence, minutes, and delayed recall in a structured log.
  4. Record decision: proceed, adjust scope, or halt before major implementation investment.
