Product requirements

Product overview

Specification: docs/01-PRD.md

Summary

  • Outcome: tier L1 (working knowledge) for each subject on the curriculum map, starting with math, electronics, and embedded software.
  • Method: short sessions, produced responses, verified items, FSRS scheduling, frontier selection, and bench projects with evidence.
  • Constraint: approximately eight hours per week for the founder, including time reserved for learning versus implementation (R-22).
  • Evidence stance: component learning mechanisms have external support; the full product combination remains to be measured in use.

Problem statement

Time

Working knowledge requires hundreds of hours per subject area; inefficient sessions have high opportunity cost.

Illusion of progress

Passive media and unguarded assistance can raise aided scores while unaided performance declines.

Retention and order

Without spacing and prerequisites, review timing and sequencing degrade.

Physical skill

Hardware competence requires observable bench outcomes, not quiz completion alone.

Concept refinements (v1.1)

  1. Progress artifacts: display the next named competence artifact and remaining requirements (FR-090, FR-091), not streaks or raw hours.
  2. Tutor scope: item-bound hint ladder and post-attempt review; no persistent cross-item chat (FR-050).
  3. Dual context: phone and desktop share FSRS state with bench workflows; simulation does not replace applied L1 requirements.
  4. Project credit: capped applied credit per contract §7.15; builds do not assign mastery in isolation.
  5. Curriculum scope: the first delivered walk covers the math / elec / embedded slice; the same loop extends to additional nodes over time.
  6. Long-term spine (v1.2): after first-walk L1, tracks for power, control, mechanical, UAV, cloud twin, and governance—see Capability spine and PRD §1.5.

Personas

P1 — Founder learner

  • Phone: micro sessions
  • Desktop: focus blocks and development tasks
  • Bench: lab blocks and project evidence
  • Also acts as reviewer and administrator until beta

P2 — Beta learner (M5)

Adult learners with five to ten hours per week; same learning loop with consent, export, deletion, and experiment assignment infrastructure.

Non-goals (version 1)

  • Native mobile applications, VR/AR, or multi-agent tutor orchestration
  • Leaderboards, badges, and social feeds
  • Neurostimulation, sleep cues, or cognitive-enhancement marketing
  • Claims of proven time savings prior to evaluation results
  • Simulation-only paths to tier L1
Open product hypotheses include daily adherence, FSRS calibration with generated variants, tutor dependence, and evidence verification quality. Milestones FR-110 and FR-111 require instrumentation from early releases.
