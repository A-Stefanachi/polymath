Hardware and tools to buy

docs/17-hardware-tools-and-bom.md, doc 10 §5 (L0–L5 USD detail), hardware-phases.yaml

Summary

  • All figures below are EUR, Germany retail incl. 19% MwSt [P], unverified planning estimates (Nov 2026).
  • Buy in phases aligned with the project ladder—avoid a full quad kit before the sensor foundation unless you accept idle stock.
  • Phase A (L0–L5): about 480–650 € for a first serious bench (DMM, scope, soldering, L0–L3 parts); up to ~665 € for the full foundation catalog.
  • Phases B–F (spine): roughly +800–1,900 € add-on over years (power bench, quad + radio, printer, companion computer).
  • Typical shops: Reichelt, Conrad, BerryBase, Amazon.de; PCBs from EU-friendly fabs.
