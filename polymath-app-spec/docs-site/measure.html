Evaluation and experiments

docs/11-evaluation-and-experiment-plan.md

Summary

  • Aided practice and strict-unaided assessments are recorded separately.
  • Exposure and outcome events support pre-registered hypotheses in doc 11.
  • Founder phase: within-subject designs (for example, tutor-enabled versus tutor-disabled intervals).
  • Beta cohort: exploratory sample; not sufficient alone to generalize time-to-L1 for all learners.

Representative outcome measures

OM-01 Delayed retention
Strict-unaided performance on skills first mastered at least eight weeks prior.
OM-04 Tutor dependence
Difference between aided practice and strict-unaided checkpoints; alert above 0.20 median gap.
OM-03 Time to criterion
Active minutes to reach a fixed target set (report-only in early phases).

Success metrics

Product-level metrics (SM-01 adherence, SM-06 zero unverified serves, SM-07 item defect rate, and others) are defined in Product overview and PRD §5. Thresholds are fixed at specification time so later results are comparable.

Claims gate

User-facing efficacy claims (for example, generalized learning speed) require support from the evaluation rubric in doc 11 §14.3. Until then, report measurements with methodology and uncertainty.
