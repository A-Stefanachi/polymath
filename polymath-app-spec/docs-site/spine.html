Cyber-physical capability spine

docs/15-cyber-physical-capability-spine.md, docs/16-capability-outcomes.md, content/capability-tracks.yaml

Summary

  • Version 1 still delivers the first walk (math, electronics, embedded) and project ladder L0–L5; the lean slice is unchanged.
  • After first-walk L1 and the go/no-go, the product grows along seven tracks toward solo cyber-physical engineering.
  • Founder goals—dual-use literacy, defense-industrial integration, production AI, cybersecurity, real-world builds—share one stack with governance boundaries (no weapon or EW design labs).
  • Project ladder L6–L13 (bench power → UAV integration → cloud twin → field V&V) is sketched, not yet fully specified.

Tracks (delivery order)

Module-level syllabi (outcomes, evidence, skill tables): Pillar syllabi viewer.

  1. T0 Foundation — first walk + sensor capstone (current MVP).
  2. T1 Power & motion — BLDC/ESC bench (L6).
  3. T2 Control — IMU ground logger (L7).
  4. T3 Mechanical — CAD frame (L9).
  5. T4 UAV integration — kit quad maiden (L8), mission slice (L12); multirotor and EU UAS ops default.
  6. T5 Cloud twin — telemetry to lakehouse (L11).
  7. T6 Governance — export-control literacy, device threat modelling, defense integration case studies (parallel with T1+).

What changes in the product later

Non-goals NG-05 and NG-06 are refined for post-v1: more nodes ship as verified content; governance and integration replace “stubs only” for defense-adjacent topics. Automated CAD/FEA grading stays out initially; CAD may use checklist and human review (doc 15).
