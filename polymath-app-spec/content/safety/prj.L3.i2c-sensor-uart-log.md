# Safety brief — Soldering and assembly (L3)

Practical lab guidance only; not medical advice. The app checks your acknowledgement, not your technique.

## Rules

- **Iron in the stand**; unplug when leaving; keep flammables away (S12).
- **Ventilation:** fume extractor or fan; open window (S13).
- **Safety glasses** for soldering and clipping leads (S14).
- **Lead hygiene:** wash hands before eating; keep solder away from food (S15).
- **Burns:** cool with running water; seek help if not minor (S16).
- **ESD** before handling boards and sensor ICs (S17).
- **Stop** if smoke, smell, or unexpected heat from the board (S19).
