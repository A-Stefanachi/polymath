# Safety brief — PCB assembly (L5)

Practical lab guidance only; not medical advice.

## Rules

- **Soldering station rules** (S12–S16): stand, ventilation, glasses, hand washing, burn first aid.
- **ESD** when handling bare boards and the Pico (S17).
- **Fibreglass boards:** do not sand or drill without dust mask and eye protection (S18).
- **First power-up:** use current-limited USB; unplug if idle current exceeds the step limit (150 mA in the project step).
- **Stop** on smoke, smell, or unexpected heat (S19).
